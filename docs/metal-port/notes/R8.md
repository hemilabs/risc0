## What was done

### `circuit/rv32im/src/prove/hal/metal/witgen.rs`
R7's CPU delegation is replaced by the GPU path. The delegation stays as the fallback.

The GPU path is the host side of CUDA `risc0_circuit_rv32im_cuda_witgen_v2` and `setup_exec` (`rv32im-sys/kernels/cuda/ffi.cu` lines 1493-1612 and 440-460). It uses `injectCycleCols` (from `misc.metal`) and the generated `par_stepExecW`, `fwd_stepExec` and `rev_stepExec` (from `witgen_tu.metal`). Steps:
- `ctx.require(Phase::Witgen)` runs first.
- **Host checks, before anything is encoded.** All of these are `ensure!` errors:
  - preflight cycles == data rows (CUDA asserts the same);
  - rows is a power of two (`RISC0_ROWS_POW2` is compiled in and Metal has no steps.cu fallback);
  - rows × cols < 2^32;
  - split ≤ cycles;
  - the pre_data shape matches data.
- **Preflight memcpy.** `ctx.upload_preflight` (R7) copies cycles | txns | bigint into the HAL-owned shared buffer. The RawPreflightCycle 36 B and RawMemoryTransaction 20 B sizes are asserted in R7's `mod.rs` and in `preflight.h`. The kernels mutate `txnIdx` in that copy, and the host trace stays pristine. An empty section is bound at the buffer start, so every binding is a valid buffer.
- **One batch:**
  1. Blit-zero the u8/u16 tables and a fresh fault word (CUDA's `cudaMemsetAsync`).
  2. `injectCycleCols`, only when the trace deferred the five per-cycle columns.
  3. In parallel mode, a `pre_data` snapshot (described below).
  4. In parallel mode, `par_stepExecW` over `[0, split)` and then `[split, lastCycle)` (CUDA's two launches), with `use_perm = 0`. Binding 10 gets the fault word as a dummy.

     In the seq modes, one 1x1 `fwd_stepExec`/`rev_stepExec` dispatch per 4096 positions, with `SeqParams{begin,end,split,lastCycle}`.

  Then `try_sync()` (the phase end, PLAN 2.6). After the sync the fault word is read. A non-zero word becomes `Err("rv32im Metal witgen (<mode>): kernel fault 0x.. (<decoded bits>)")`, which the prover wraps as "witness generation failure".
- **Param structs.** `WitgenParams` (48 B), `SeqParams` (16 B) and `InjectParams` (32 B) are `#[repr(C)] bytemuck::NoUninit` structs whose sizes are asserted. The offsets are tested against the MSL static_asserts.
- **pre_data.** PLAN 4.2 asks for a real buffer by default. The prover passes `data` itself as `pre_data`, unless `RISC0_USE_PRE_DATA` is set.
  - When they alias, a copy of `data` is taken after `injectCycleCols` and bound at index 1 (`hal.eltwise_copy_elem`, in the same batch).
  - A separate `pre_data` from the prover is bound as it is: it already holds the scatter and the cycle columns.
  - `RISC0_METAL_WITGEN_SINGLE_BUFFER=1` binds `data` itself, which is CUDA's default.
  - The seq kernels ignore binding 1 (R5).
- **Allocation errors.** If an allocation of witgen's own (the preflight copy, the tables, the fault word, the snapshot) panics in the HAL, the panic is caught and returned as an `Err`. The HAL panics when the `RISC0_METAL_MAX_WORKING_SET_BYTES` budget is exceeded. The snapshot error also names the single-buffer knob.
- **Flags.** `writes_cycle_cols()` and `takes_zeroed_data()` both return true, so data is zero-filled and not zeroized afterwards. Both are false when witgen runs on the CPU fallback.
- **CPU fallback.** `RISC0_METAL_RV32IM_CPU_PHASES` is a comma list. If it names `witgen`, R7's delegation runs (`cpu_witgen`). The list is read once per process. `cpu_phase(name)` is `pub(crate)`, so the sibling modules can call it as `super::witgen::cpu_phase` and R9 and R10 parse the same variable.
- **Tests.** The test module is `witgen_tests.rs`, declared from witgen.rs with `#[path]`, so `mod.rs` did not need a `mod` line.

### Other files
- `hal/metal/mod.rs`: one append-only line, `pub(crate) use witgen::defers_cycle_cols;`.
- `prove/witgen/mod.rs`: `DEFER_CYCLE_COLS` gets a metal arm. It equals `enabled("rv32im") && !cpu_phase("witgen")`.
  - With a Metal witgen, the host injector leaves the five per-cycle columns out and `injectCycleCols` writes them, as on CUDA.
  - On the CPU prover, or with the CPU fallback, `writes_cycle_cols` is false, so the host scatters them, as before.
- `prove/witgen/tests.rs`: the commented-out Metal arm of `fwd_rev_ab_test` is replaced by a real `feature = "metal"` arm (`Arc<MetalHalPoseidon2>` + `MetalCircuitHalPoseidon2`).

## Open issue: intermittent wrong results under GPU contention
In 4 of 15 full-suite runs (`prove::` or the whole lib; 2 of 7 at 4 GiB, 2 of 3 serial runs without a budget, 0 in the rest), and in 1 of 2 runs of `witgen_matches_cpu_riscv` with a second process loading the GPU, one Metal witgen call went wrong:
- 4 times in total, `kernel fault 0x2` (lookup index out of range) in parallel mode, on traces that passed on every other rep (slli twice, srli, srai).
- Once, **3 wrong cells with no fault**, in the single-threaded reverse kernel: andi po2 13, row 1293, cols 40 and 147, cpu `0xf00f` vs metal `0x700f` (one bit). The parallel reps and the fwd pass of the same trace were correct.

It was never seen in ~13 runs with less machine load: 4 × hal::metal under 4 GB, 4 × without a budget, 3 × riscv with 5 reps, and 3 × large+riscv. Each of those covers 46 × (3 to 5 parallel + 2 seq) witgen calls.

Because one case was in a single-thread kernel, a thread race inside `par_stepExecW` cannot be the whole explanation. A load-dependent corruption of inputs or outputs fits all cases. Candidates not yet ruled out:
- (a) A pool/sync hazard in the zkp runtime: a shared buffer reused or host-written while a committed batch still reads it. I read `release`/`reclaim`/`open_batch`/`poll`/`small_scatter` and found no bug.
- (b) GPU preemption/context switches of the deep noinline call graph.

Root cause unknown. The fault word catches some cases; the silent mismatch shows that not all are caught. **R11 / Z8 / R15 must reproduce this before relying on Metal witgen output.** Reproduce with `witgen_matches_cpu_riscv` and `RISC0_METAL_WITGEN_REPS=5`, while a second process runs `witgen_matches_cpu_large` with `RISC0_METAL_WITGEN_PO2S=20,21`. Logs are in `/private/tmp/claude-501/-Users-user-zkminer/a30bccea-eec8-43d4-ac51-c5ccb6e4b957/scratchpad/R8/` (`freq_none_2.log`, `freq_none_3.log`, `budget4g_rerun_1.log`, `underload_2.log`). Suggested next steps:
- Dump the data buffer before witgen when a fault occurs, to separate bad input (scatter/fill) from a bad kernel result.
- Test the same load on R7's CPU delegation, which shares the scatter/fill path, to see whether only the kernels are affected.

## Assumed / gaps
- Cold PSO creation for the three witgen kernels is ≈ 6.5 s (R5). Under the machine's current contention, one run waited ~22 min on `MTLCompilerService` before starting: warm-up (M1/K5) matters.
- The CPU oracle is the CPU HAL in SeqForward mode. CUDA's par path is not available here for a three-way check.
- `defers_cycle_cols()` uses `enabled("rv32im")` at first use of `DEFER_CYCLE_COLS`. If a process changes `RISC0_METAL`/`RISC0_METAL_SKIP` after the first preflight, the injector and the HAL can disagree. That stays correct anyway: if the HAL does not write the columns, the host scatters them.
- The tables are zeroed per witgen call. Accum (R9) zeroes its own, as CUDA `setup_accum` does.

## Issues for later items
- **R7/mod.rs owner (R14):** `ScratchBuffers` sits behind a `Mutex`, and `grow()` allocates while holding it. A budget panic inside `upload_preflight`/`tables` poisons that Mutex, so every later phase of that circuit HAL panics on `lock().unwrap()`. Use `lock().unwrap_or_else(PoisonError::into_inner)` or fallible allocation. R8 converts the panic to an `Err` but cannot un-poison.
- **F0/Z6 (zkp HAL):** there is no fallible `MetalHal` allocation API. R8 uses `catch_unwind` around `alloc_*`. A `try_alloc_buffer` would be cleaner.
- **R9/R10:** use `witgen::cpu_phase("accum"/"eval")` for `RISC0_METAL_RV32IM_CPU_PHASES`. Witgen leaves `pf` with mutated `txnIdx`, so accum must re-upload (`upload_preflight`), as CUDA's `setup_accum` re-copies.
- **R11:** the intermittent issue above. Also, `segment_prover()`'s debug log still says "circuit kernels on CPU until R8/R9/R10" (mod.rs is R7's, left unchanged).
- **R15:** the single-buffer mode matched the CPU in every run here (simple_loop po2 17, 3 reps per run). Dropping the snapshot halves witgen's peak (−885 MB at po2 20) and lets po2 22 fit in 4 GB.
- **M-items:** the witgen phase peak is ≈ 2 × rows × 211 × 4 B plus small buffers: 1.9 GB at po2 20 and 7.5 GB at po2 22.
