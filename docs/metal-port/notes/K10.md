## Changes

### #51 rightsize po2 (`exec/syscall/keccak.rs`)

`request_po2(inputs, max_po2, rightsize)` gives the smallest po2 in `14..max_po2` with
`max_keccak_inputs(po2) >= inputs`, else `max_po2`. It never goes above the max, and it uses the same
capacity function as the guest's batch-full signal. The claim digest does not depend on the po2. The lift
uses that po2's keccak zkr, whose control ID `KECCAK_CONTROL_IDS[po2-14]` is under
`KECCAK_CONTROL_ROOT`, so the receipt verifies the same way. `ProveKeccak` `metric.size` now reports
2^(the po2 actually proved). No zk-miner consumer reads it (grep).

After the change (bin_B): prove 157 ms + lift 185 ms, keccak total ~365 ms.

### #53 one keccak prover per session (`prove/keccak.rs` `prove_and_lift_keccaks`, `prover_impl.rs`)

On Metal (`cfg!(feature = "metal")`), the session's keccak requests now run in two phases:
1. One keccak prover proves every seal and checks each claim digest.
2. The prover is dropped, then every seal is lifted.

So no lift runs beside the keccak prover's pool. Seals are small (~10^5 words) and the inputs are already
held by `session.pending_keccaks`. Other backends keep the old per-request loop: on Intel/AMD it overlaps
host witnesses, and CUDA is unmeasured. The knob restores that loop on Metal.

### #57 verify overlap (opt-in)

With `RISC0_KECCAK_VERIFY_OVERLAP=1`, each seal's CPU verification runs on a thread while the GPU proves
the next seal. At most one verifier thread runs at a time: the previous one is joined first. The thread
is detached rather than scoped, so a proof error cannot turn into a scope panic. All seals are verified
before any lift. It is off by default because its win is within noise (below).

Reading the numbers:
- Reuse wins 130-200 ms. That is more than A's own spread (67-100 ms), so it is kept.
- Overlap wins 30 ms, which is less than A's spread, so it counts as "no change" and is opt-in.
- Metal peak (11936 MiB) and RSS are the same in every arm. The segment proof dominates the peak, and the
  two-phase order adds nothing measurable.

Budgets for union: the `union:3` job fails identically on A and B at both 4 GiB and 8 GiB.
- The error is `Metal working-set budget exceeded: allocating 3539992576 bytes for evaluated`.
- Cause: the test harness proves the multi-test guest at its default segment po2 without budget-driven
  segment sizing.
- So this is outside K10, and the 4/8 GiB budgets are covered by the `small` job only.

## Open issues

- **Mixed-po2 sessions under #53 are not measured.** One prover now proves requests of different po2s, and
  its pool may hold buffers for several sizes at once. The worst case is about the sum of po2 14..max
  buffers, under 2x the pre-K10 max-po2 set. The `union` and `small` jobs are single-po2.
  - Mitigation if this turns out to matter: rebuild the prover when the po2 changes. `keccak_prover()` costs
    ~0.1 ms once warm.
- **The coprocessor/API path** (one callback per request) still makes one prover per request.
- **#56** (keep the recursion provers across keccak at large budgets) is deferred: not started.
- **#54** (uint4 poly_mix loads) and **R20-style specialization** of the keccak eval stages are deferred:
  not started, and their LEADS microbenchmarks were not run. After #51 the 1-batch job's keccak share is
  ~157 ms of prove, so any kernel win is bounded at a few tens of ms per request. They need ir_scan and
  run_stress on keccak-sys.
- **CUDA** keeps the old loop: two-phase is gated to Metal until someone measures it on CUDA.
- **Budget coverage for `union:3`:** the 4/8 GiB budgets could not be tested on that job because of the
  test harness's segment po2 (see above).
