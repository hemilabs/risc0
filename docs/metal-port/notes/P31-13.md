  change regenerates every lift program's control ID, the keccak lifts included. The keccak *seal* is
  unchanged: the keccak circuit, verifier and Metal kernels are all the same. The lifted receipt's
  control ID is not, so the zkvm e2e tests in P31-14 are the gate for keccak receipts verifying with the
  stock 3.1 verifier and for 3.0 keccak receipts being rejected. Nothing in the keccak crate can test that.
