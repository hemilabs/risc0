## Translation (source lines -> output)

Inputs are the checked-in zirgen outputs in `kernels/cuda`: `step_exec.cu` (53092 lines: `step_exec`
:97-115 plus 19 `__noinline__` arms, declared :19-95 and defined :117-53091), `step_verify_mem.cu`
(4522), `step_compute_accum.cu` (15627), `step_verify_accum.cu` (8302). `step_verify_bytes.cu` is
skipped (empty body, no caller). The `_inl.cuh` copies are not used (plan: Inl variants unneeded).

Every rewrite is a line-local `Rule` whose output spelling must not already occur in the input:

| Rule | CUDA | MSL | step_exec | verify_mem | compute_accum | verify_accum |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| include | `#include "extern.cuh"` | `#include "extern.h"` (C1, kernels/metal) | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| qual.noinline | `__device__ __noinline__ void` | `static __attribute__((noinline)) void` | **38** | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| qual.device_fn | `__device__ void step_*(` | `static inline void step_*(` | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| ctx_param | `void* ctx,` | `thread ExecCtx* ctx,` / `thread AccumCtx* ctx,` | 39 | 1 | 1 | 1 |
| arg_param | `Fp* argN` (params) | `device Fp* argN` | 119 | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| device_auto_ref | `auto& reg = argN[..]` | `device auto& reg = ...` | **1768** | 250 | 0 | 92 |
| assert | `assert(E);` | `ctx->bad \|= !(E);` | **4373** | **960** (240 with an `&& "eqz failed..."` message, kept: MSL accepts it, `-Wall` clean) | 0 | 0 |
| fp_zero | `xN = 0;` | `xN = Fp(0);` | **51** | 0 | 0 | 0 |
| trap | `if (c1_bad) __trap();` | `ctx->bad \|= c1_bad;` | 0 | 0 | **1** | **1** |
| tally.c1_bad (passes through) | `c1_bad` tokens | same | 0 | 0 | **486** | **329** |
| tally.extern_call (passes through) | `extern_*(ctx, cycle, "..", extern_args, extern_outs)` | same; binds C1's `(thread *Ctx*, uint32_t, constant char*, thread Fp*, thread Fp*)` | 275 | 47 | 13 | 13 |
| wrapper (generated block) | — | one kernel | 1 | 1 | 1 | 1 |

These are the `PINNED` dict; every plan-pinned number (51 Fp(0), 4373+960 asserts, 1768 device
auto&, 38 noinline, 486+329 c1_bad, 2 trap) matches. Everything else (Fp arithmetic, `Fp extern_args[96]`
thread arrays, `Fp(a.asUInt32() & b.asUInt32())`, `x != 0` against an implicit `Fp(uint32_t)`,
`inv`, reads `argN[k*steps + ((cycle - d) & mask)]`, writes `arg1[k] = x` into globals) compiles
unchanged against F1's fp.h. A residual scan refuses `__device__`/`__noinline__`/`__trap`/`void* ctx`/
unqualified `auto&` or `Fp*`/`assert(`/`xN = 0;`/CUDA headers/`size_t`/`printf` in the output.

Generated text without a CUDA counterpart (a prelude comment +
`#pragma clang diagnostic ignored "-Wunused-variable"` before the include, the kernel at the end)
sits between `// ---- cuda2metal: generated (begin|end) ----` lines.

### Kernel wrappers (ported from ffi.cu)

| Kernel | CUDA | Bindings (C1 context.h order) | Body |
|---|---|---|---|
| `parStepExec` (in step_exec.metal) | ffi.cu:198-217 (`nextStepExec` + `parStepExec`) | 0 ctrl, 1 data, 2 global, 3 wom, 4 cycles, 5 iops, 6 womRows, 7 womIndex, 8 fault, 9 `RecExecParams` | `gid >= numCycles` or `isParSafe == 2` → return; `make_exec_ctx`; row 0 or a parallel-safe row runs itself and the following `isParSafe == 0` rows; `r0_fault_flush(fault, ctx.bad)`. Step args `ctrl, global, data, nullptr, nullptr` as CUDA. |
| `parStepVerifyWom` (step_verify_mem.metal) | ffi.cu:754-761 | same as parStepExec | `step_verify_mem` for `gid < numCycles`; flush. |
| `parStepComputeAccum` (step_compute_accum.metal) | ffi.cu:807-819 | 0 ctrl, 1 global, 2 data, 3 mix, 4 accum (group), 5 accumTmp (FpExt scratch), 6 fault, 7 `RecAccumParams` | `gid < workCycles`; `make_accum_ctx`; step args ctrl, global, data, mix, accum; flush. |
| `parStepVerifyAccum` (step_verify_accum.metal) | ffi.cu:821-833 | same as compute accum | same with `step_verify_accum`. |

All four carry `[[max_total_threads_per_threadgroup(256)]]` before `kernel void` (TG 256; sweep is
C11), use `thread_position_in_grid` (dispatch as `Grid::Threads(n)`), and are at global scope. Added
beyond CUDA (plan 2.9): `numCycles > totalCycles` (exec) / `workCycles > totalCycles` (accum) → thread
0 sets fault 1 and every thread returns, since the step code indexes `col * steps + cycle`.
`--define C2M_PSO_NONCE=N` adds one never-taken compare on a params pad word (only for cold PSO timing).

## Assumed / not verified / gaps

- **No functional GPU-vs-CPU test of the step kernels** in C2 (needs a real recursion trace, the
  preflight, CPU sort/scan between the exec and verify passes: C6/C7/C9). Only compile, PSO and the
  empty/fault-guard smoke were run. The translation is a byte-exact round trip of the CUDA text, so
  correctness rests on fp.h/fpext.h (F1) and C1's externs matching their CUDA counterparts.
- `assert` semantics differ by design: CUDA aborts the grid on the first failure; here every thread
  runs to completion with possibly invalid values and the host sees `fault != 0` after the batch.
- The 19 arms stay `noinline` (as in CUDA). Whether inlining (or the HIP-style split) is faster on
  Apple GPUs, and occupancy of the 19-arm kernel, are C11 questions; the cold PSO cost is low enough
  that neither form is constrained by it.
- The `C2M_PSO_NONCE` block is the only code that is not a 1:1 port; it is compiled out unless
  defined.
