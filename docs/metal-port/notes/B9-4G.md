# B9-4G: Groth16 at the 4 GiB budget on 3.1 (16 GB Macs), in every order

Branch `metal/B9-4G` (worktree `/Users/user/zkminer/deps/risc0-3.1-wt-B9-4G`), on `metal/P31-G1` `f6a661864`.
Wave 9 (beta, no performance work). Release blocker: at a 4 GiB working-set budget the 3.1 Groth16 key load
was refused once the keccak pipeline objects existed (after any keccak proof, or after zk-miner's
`warm_up_pipelines`), so a 16 GB Mac at the 4 GiB tier could not make Groth16 receipts. Read: PLAN 2, 4.5, 5, 8
(8.7), 9; BASELINES '3.1'; notes P31-14 (open issue 1), P31-12, B9-G1, zk-miner P31-M9b (open issue 3).

Files (all owned): `risc0/groth16-sys/src/metal/{prover.rs, prover_tests.rs, ntt.rs, msm.rs, mod.rs}`,
`tools/stock_verify31/run_matrix.sh`, this note. No Metal kernel source changed (the fix reuses `ntt_mul_powers`
with other parameters), so `ir_scan.py` / `run_stress.sh` (R17) do not apply. No zk-miner change is needed.

Scratch: `/Users/user/zkminer/scratch/w9/B9-4G/` (`logs/`, `head/` headroom runs, `ab/{b4g,b8g,bdef}` A/B,
`matrix/` stock-verifier matrix, `harness{A,B}/` the producer copied with two additions, `ab.sh`, `ana.py`,

## Profile before

Reproduced P31-14's failure with the unmodified producer (`logs/before_wpso_4g.log`, `P14_PREBUILD=worker`,
4 GiB): `allocating 4235118872 bytes for the Groth16 prover ... with 64946176 bytes already allocated would
exceed the limit of 4294967296`. Allocation trace: 29.9 MiB after the warmup, 61.9 MiB after the composite and
succinct proofs and `release_hal_buffers` (process-wide pipeline state in 16 MiB steps, never freed).

Where the 4 GiB check stood (P31-12 `logs/final_g10_split.log`, re-measured here in `b9_4g_tw_lent_bit_exact`):
- planned 4038.9 MiB, real growth 4008.0 MiB: the check carried **30.9 MiB more** than the prover allocates. That
  is `PLAN_SLACK` = 32 MiB, sized for `RISC0_G16_METAL_ARCHIVE=0`, where every key load builds the NTT plan's own
  pipelines after the check. With the archive (default since G11) the plan builds nothing; the real excess over
  the bare buffer sum is page rounding, measured 1.1 MiB.
- Real peak with the best PSO state (45.9 MiB): 4054 MiB = 42 MiB headroom; with the keccak PSOs the check fails.

So making the check exact gives back ~31 MiB (enough for 61.9 MiB, 26 MiB of real headroom, measured below),
but not the >= 128 MiB the item asks for. That needs ~106 MiB less real allocation at 4 GiB. Breakdown of the
split layout (P31-12): points 2421, compact coefficients 343, a/b 512, witness 191, NTT tables 128, MSM scratch
~412 MiB. Candidates:
- points, coefficients, a/b, witness: live for the whole proof (or reloaded per proof: tens of ms).
- **NTT twiddle table (n/2 Fr = 128 MiB)**: used only in the NTT stage. The MSM scratch's `sorted` buffer
  (16 windows x 6,257,165 x 4 B = 381.9 MiB) holds c during that stage (G10 alias, 256 MiB) and its tail
  (125.9 MiB) is free until MSM H's sort. 2.1 MiB short of the table (P31-12's open issue noted it).

## Change 1: the up-front check equals the real growth (+ <= 4 MiB)

`prover.rs::plan_slack()`: 4 MiB with the pipeline archive (page rounding), P31-12's 32 MiB without it.
Knob `RISC0_G16_METAL_PLAN_SLACK_MIB=<n>` (32 = the old check). Measured in `b9_4g_tw_lent_bit_exact` (asserted:
planned - growth in [0, 4 MiB]):

| layout | planned | real growth | planned - growth |
|---|---|---|---|
| whole (default, 8 GiB) | 4141.0 MiB | 4138.1 | 2.9 |
| split H | 4010.9 | 4008.0 | 2.9 |
| whole + lent twiddles | 4013.0 | 4010.1-4010.3 | 2.7-2.9 |
| split + lent twiddles (4 GiB) | 3885.0 | 3882.1-3882.3 | 2.7-2.9 |

Alone (`RISC0_G16_METAL_TW_LENT=off`, `head/knob_off.log`): the 4 GiB wrap after the worker warmup passes the
check, peak 4070.0 MiB, **26 MiB headroom**. Too thin to rely on, so not the fix on its own.

## Change 2: `MemOptions::tw_lent`, the NTT twiddles in the MSM scratch (the 4 GiB layout)

- `ntt.rs`: `NttPlan::with_lent_twiddles(.., Some((buffer, offset)))` keeps the twiddle table in a caller's
  region instead of an own 128 MiB buffer (offset bound with `set_buffer`, kernels unchanged). The region is
  rebuilt before each use: the caller fills it with the byte 0x01 (`LENT_FILL_BYTE`; every limb 0x01010101 is
  a canonical Montgomery value K) and `encode_lent_twiddles` runs one `ntt_mul_powers` pass over n/2 entries
  with two small owned tables (lo = w^k, k < 2^12; hi = K^-1 w^(k 2^12), 1024 entries; 160 KiB), so entry j
  = K * w^(j mod 4096) * K^-1 w^(4096 (j >> 12)) = w^j, canonical, bit for bit the owned table.
- `msm.rs`: `MsmScratch::new_with_sorted(.., min_sorted)`: `sorted` grows to hold c (n Fr) and the table
  (n/2 Fr): 384.0 MiB instead of 381.9.
- `prover.rs`: with `tw_lent` the scratch is built first and the plan lends `sorted` at offset n * 32 (behind c).
  Each proof's first command buffer fills the region (same blit as a, b's zero fill); the NTT stage rebuilds the
  table first. witness_poly writes only c (before it); MSM H's sort, after the NTT stage, is the first writer of
  the rest of `sorted`. No allocation during a proof (asserted).
- **Layout choice** (`choose_layout`, under `Auto`): candidates fastest first: whole; split H; whole + lent;
  split + lent. Whole if it fits the room (P31-12's rule: default and 8 GiB unchanged); else the first smaller
  one that leaves `AUTO_HEADROOM_BYTES` (128 MiB, twice the largest pipeline set measured) of the room; else the
  smallest. At a 4 GiB budget (room 4034-4050 MiB) that is split + lent in every order; at 8 GiB and the default
  budget whole. With `tw_lent` off it is P31-12's rule exactly.
- Knobs: `RISC0_G16_METAL_TW_LENT=on|auto|off` (default `auto`), `RISC0_G16_METAL_G10_OFF=tw_lent` (= off),
  `RISC0_G16_METAL_G10=0` (G9 layout), `MemOptions::parse("tw_lent=..")`. `BuildStats::tw_lent`,
  `MetalProver::tw_lent()` report the decision. `RISC0_G16_METAL_TW_LENT=off` + `RISC0_G16_METAL_PLAN_SLACK_MIB=32`
  is the base behaviour exactly (refused after the warmup, `head/knob_off_slack32.log`). Forcing `on` forces
  only the twiddles: with split H on `auto` it takes whole + lent at 4 GiB (peak 4072 MiB, `knob_on.log`).

### Tried and rejected: rebuilding the table with `ntt_powers`

The first version rebuilt the lent table per proof with the plan's own `ntt_powers` kernel (up to 22
multiplications per entry). In-process `g10_bench` (real v0.2.0 witness, 9 reps, split H on, variants
off/on/off interleaved, `logs/g10bench_prof{0,1}.log`): **+9.4 ms GPU per proof** (ntt stage 82.7 -> 92.7 ms
profiled). Replaced by the fill + `ntt_mul_powers` rebuild (2 multiplications per entry).

### Cost of the kept version (`logs/g10bench2_prof{0,1}.log`, same setup)

|---|---|---|---|
| split, own twiddles (A) | 610.5 [607.5..611.8] | 82.8 | 15.7 [12.5..16.1] |

The rebuild costs ~0.8 ms of the ntt stage; the fill hides in the zero stage. Totals: inside A-vs-A. The key load
is faster (no 128 MiB table to build: gpu setup 24.7 -> 6.8-7.9 ms).

## Memory and budgets

- Device: 4 GiB -125.8 MiB at the wrap peak (all orders above); 8 GiB and default unchanged (4142.1 MiB prover).
- Process footprint at 4 GiB (`wrap_under_4gib_budget`, A/B x3): 3984.0-3984.2 -> **3858.4-3859.3 MiB**
  (-125.6 MiB; A's first run 4583 MiB). Default and 8 GiB: same layout, no change. No peak rises anywhere.
- `planned_bytes()` (used by the par_transient gate and the early key load) is 28 MiB lower at 8 GiB / default
  (4141.0 vs 4169.0 MiB) and 154 MiB lower at 4 GiB. Neither decision flips: the gate needs limit - planned >=
  1280 MiB (8 GiB: 4051 -> par_transient as before; 4 GiB: 211 -> fast as before), and the early load needs
  identity_p254 below 2 GiB (unchanged at 4 GiB: never).
- Smallest budget that builds the prover: ~3885 MiB + the pipeline objects (was ~3.95 GiB).
- PLAN 9.1: no core counts or RAM fractions; the layout follows the working-set limit and the current
  allocation; `AUTO_HEADROOM_BYTES` is a byte margin.

## Open issues

1. **zk-miner (B9-ZK): no change needed for this fix.** The worker's start-up `warm_up_pipelines` (rv32im,
   recursion, keccak, Groth16 PSOs) can stay as it is: the 4 GiB wrap now has >= 151 MiB of headroom after it.
   What the worker must keep doing at the 4 GiB tier: release the Groth16 key between wraps (`release_srs`, done
   below 20 GiB per B9-G1). Two wraps without a release still fail at 4 GiB, as on 3.0 (P31-14 open issue 2;
   re-checked: with the 3882 MiB key cached, the second identity_p254's 12 MiB allocation does not fit,
   `head/h_B_norelease.log`). A worker log line with `BuildStats::split_h` / `tw_lent` at its 4 GiB tier would
   confirm the layout in the field.
2. Not measured in the zk-miner worker itself (`BENCH_ONLY`): the producer's `P14_PREBUILD=worker` stands in for
   its warmup, and the wrap A/B is at the library level. The STARK phases are untouched.
3. The first wrap of a process at 4 GiB may be up to ~8 ms slower (above); later wraps are not. Not explained.
4. `planned_bytes()` still depends on the room at the moment it is asked (P31-12 / G13 behaviour): asked while a
   prover is cached at 8 GiB it describes a reduced layout. Its two callers ask before a build; the test that
   asked after one is fixed.
5. `AUTO_HEADROOM_BYTES` (128 MiB) only decides between the split layout and split + lent twiddles; a budget
   where even the smallest layout leaves less still builds it (and the check refuses below ~3.8 GiB + PSOs).
6. Not measured on a real 16 GB Mac; the 4 GiB tier is simulated with `RISC0_METAL_MAX_WORKING_SET_BYTES` on this
   Mac (PLAN 9).
